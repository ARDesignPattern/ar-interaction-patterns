Patterns

A reusable interaction design pattern system for HMD-based AR.

AR interaction design patterns provide structured solutions to recurring challenges in augmented reality interaction design. Grounded in empirical research and iterative prototyping, the pattern system captures reusable design knowledge for navigation, spatial interaction, content presentation, and visitor experience in museum and cultural heritage contexts.
