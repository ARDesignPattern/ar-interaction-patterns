This study examines how HMD-based interaction can be evaluated in real museum environments where visitors are often first-time users and interaction is embedded in public exhibition spaces.

## Research Focus

The work focuses on methods for observing both in-headset interaction and the visitor's physical behavior without interrupting the museum experience.

## Evaluation Approach

The HMD view was streamed to an external monitor while researchers observed visitor behavior and recorded interaction events using structured observation materials.

## Context

The approach was applied repeatedly across museum-oriented XR prototypes and field deployments.
