---
title: "Walk-up XR for Real-World Environments"
---

This study investigates how HMD-based AR experiences can support first-time museum visitors through intuitive interaction, spatial guidance, and walk-up use without extensive onboarding.

## Research Focus

The study explores interaction design challenges that emerge when visitors encounter an HMD-based AR experience directly in a real museum environment.

## Context

The work was developed through iterative prototyping and field deployment in museum and cultural heritage settings.
