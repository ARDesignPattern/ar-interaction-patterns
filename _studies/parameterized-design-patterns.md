This study investigates how interaction design knowledge for museum XR can be abstracted into reusable and adaptable design patterns.

## Research Focus

The work examines how recurring interaction strategies can be formalized so that they remain understandable, reusable, and transferable across different exhibition contexts.

## Two-Level Pattern System

The resulting system distinguishes between application-level patterns and higher-level category patterns.

Application-level patterns describe concrete interaction solutions, while category-level patterns provide broader structures and parameters for adapting and deriving interaction solutions.

## Context

The pattern system was developed through iterative design, prototyping, deployment, and expert review.
