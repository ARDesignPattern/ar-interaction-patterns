This study investigates how game-mechanic-based interaction can support engagement, exploration, and learning in HMD-based museum experiences.

## Research Focus

The work explores how playful interaction strategies can be integrated into museum XR without turning the experience into a conventional game.

## Design Exploration

The study includes interaction concepts based on puzzle solving, drawing, object manipulation, and action-based tasks.

## Context

The concepts were explored through iterative prototypes and museum-oriented XR scenarios.
