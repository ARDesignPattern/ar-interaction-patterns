This study examines how the spatial placement of 3D user interfaces influences interaction and experience in museum AR.

## Research Focus

The work investigates how different anchoring and placement strategies affect usability, cognitive load, and the relationship between digital content and physical exhibits.

## Evaluation

Different spatial UI configurations were evaluated with museum-oriented HMD-AR prototypes.

## Context

The study contributes empirical evidence for deciding where interface elements should be positioned within spatial museum experiences.
