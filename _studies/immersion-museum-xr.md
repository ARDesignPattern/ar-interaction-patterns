This study investigates different forms of immersion in an HMD-based museum experience.

## Research Focus

The work compares flow, narrative immersion, and spatial immersion to better understand how different design strategies influence engagement and learning.

## Comparative Study

The study examines how different experience configurations shape visitors' subjective experience and interaction with museum content.

## Context

The findings contribute to a more differentiated understanding of immersion as a design goal in museum XR.
